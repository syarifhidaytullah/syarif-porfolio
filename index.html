hr_attrition_analysis.sql 
-- Analisis Lembur vs Turnover Karyawan
SELECT 
  d.department_name,
  e.overtime,
  COUNT(*) AS total_karyawan,
  ROUND(AVG(e.monthly_income), 0) AS avg_gaji
FROM employees e
JOIN departments d 
  ON e.department_id = d.department_id
GROUP BY d.department_name, e.overtime;
-- → OverTime attrition 30.5% vs non-OT 10.4%

Prinsip Analisis

  1. Kritik Sumber & Data Lineage — pemisahan tegas antara data input, kalkulasi helper, dan summary dashboard agar rumus selalu transparan dan dapat diaudit.
